Pacific Coast Road Trip — Itinerary

Aug 8–15 · 8 days, 7 nights · ~1,550 miles · Seattle → LA, skipping the Olympic Peninsula. Three of us, Hertz car, cheap lodging. Extending to Aug 15 bought a full Mount Rainier day and a full LA day — you still sleep in a bed every night, and nobody races the beach day to a flight anymore.

Flights & car — both booked
  • Out · Sat Aug 8: DL2534 SAT 7:30 AM → LAX 8:33 AM, then DL1045 LAX 12:07 PM → land SEA 2:50 PM.
  • Back · Sat Aug 15 (booked): DL2568 LAX 10:59 AM → SAT 3:59 PM. Car goes back FRIDAY 9 PM — Saturday is hotel-shuttle only (~8:30 AM, airside ~9).
  • 🚗 Hertz: picked up SEA ~3:45 PM Aug 8; drop is LAX Friday Aug 14 by 9 PM (7 days — verify adjusted total). All three drivers on the contract.

🌊 Water safety — read before you get in anything

You cannot swim the northern half of this trip. August water temps: Oregon coast 52–59°F, San Francisco ~58°F. Wetsuit-only.

Sneaker waves on the Oregon/NorCal coast surge 150+ feet up the beach with no warning and have killed 24+ people on the West Coast since 2012. The cold takes your limb control within seconds, and the waves carry sand/gravel that weighs your clothes down.

Rules for Cannon Beach, Boardman, and the Redwood beaches:

  • Never turn your back on the ocean
  • Stay off and away from beach logs (they roll and pin people)
  • Keep well up the sand — wading is fine, swimming is not

Swimmable water starts in SoCal: Santa Monica/Malibu ~69°F, lifeguarded. That's Day 7, and it's where all three water activities live.

⚠ Kayak vs. Big Sur — you can't do both Wednesday

A 3–5 PM paddle at Moss Landing puts you at Point Lobos at 5:30, which means driving Big Sur in the dark. Pick one:

  • BRANCH A (recommended — keeps everything): Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk 12:30–2:15 to fill the gap (free entry, the 1924 Giant Dipper, actual beach) → 🌊 kayak 3–5 → Salinas ~6 PM → Big Sur tomorrow, unhurried, 6:45 start.
  • BRANCH B (drop the kayak): straight to Point Lobos ~12:45 and run Big Sur this afternoon in good light → Pismo ~8:30 PM. Frees Thursday entirely — but cancel the kayak so you're not charged.

Apple Park reality check: the ring building and campus are closed to the public. The Visitor Center gives you an AR model on an iPad, a roof terrace with a distant view, a café, and exclusive merch. That's it — 45 minutes covers it.
