Say this when ordering

  • Vegetarian: “No meat, fish, shellfish, fish sauce, oyster sauce, shrimp paste, animal broth, or lard.” Thai and Mexican kitchens especially need this clarification.
  • Other two: choose only chicken, shrimp, fish, or mutton. Avoid bacon, chorizo, beef stock, pork-based beans, and mixed-meat sauces. The selected budget stops focus on chicken and seafood because no dependable low-cost mutton stop was found directly on this route.
  • If cross-contact matters for allergy or religious reasons—not just preference—call ahead. These are mixed kitchens.

The daily plan

Every primary stop is on or very close to the existing route. Backups prioritize speed so the sightseeing schedule survives.

Day 1 · Aug 8 · Seattle early dinner

Pike Place Chowder

1530 Post Alley · daily 11 AM–5 PM
$10–20 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Lime & Coconut Chowder, fully vegan and gluten-free: $9.95 small, about $12.95 large; vegan sourdough bread bowl +$7.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Smoked Salmon Chowder or Seafood Bisque. Avoid New England chowder (bacon) and Crab & Oyster chowder (chorizo).

Timing: the flight lands 2:50 PM and downtown arrival is ~4:30 — Post Alley closes at 5, so preorder from the road for a ~4:40 pickup. If you miss the window, the Pacific Place location typically stays open later (confirm same day), or eat chowder as the early half of dinner and top up downtown before the Ashford drive.

Day 1 · Aug 8 · grocery run for Rainier

Safeway (Graham/Eatonville) or a downtown Seattle market

On WA-161 en route to Ashford · most locations open until 11 PM or later
$12–18 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Tomorrow's breakfast and trail lunch: instant oatmeal or bakery items, bananas, PB&J or hummus-and-veg wraps, trail mix, granola bars, cheese sticks. Check granola bars for gelatin if that matters.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Same trail staples plus tuna packets or pre-made chicken wraps from the deli case. Skip anything with bacon bits, ham, or mixed-meat deli salads.

Do not skip this: nothing in Ashford opens before 7 AM and there is NO food or gas inside the park early — tomorrow's 6 AM gate entry means breakfast, trail lunch, snacks, and plenty of water all come from tonight's bag. Fill the gas tank at the same stop.

Day 2 · Aug 9 · on the mountain

Packed trail food + Copper Creek Inn pie

Skyline Trail all morning · Copper Creek Inn, WA-706 in Ashford, on the way out ~5:30 PM
Mostly packed · pie ~$8–10
🌱 Two vegetarians

Last night's groceries cover breakfast at the motel and lunch at Panorama Point. The famous Copper Creek wild blackberry pie is vegetarian — the reward stop after the hike.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Same packed food on the trail. If ordering a real meal at Copper Creek, stick to chicken or trout dishes and skip the beef and pork plates.

Optional hot food in the park: the Paradise Inn dining room and the Tatoosh Cafe in the Jackson Visitor Center at Paradise serve limited hours in summer — treat them as a bonus, not the plan. Copper Creek at ~5:30 PM works only if you leave the park on schedule.

Day 2 · Aug 9 · Astoria dinner

Fort George Brewery (as a restaurant)

1483 Duane Street, Astoria · kitchen hours vary by room — confirm it serves past 8:30 PM
$14–22 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Wood-fired cheese or veggie pizza, veggie burger, or salads. Confirm no anchovy, bacon, or meat broth in the pick.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Fish and chips or a fried chicken sandwich. Avoid the beef burgers and any pork-topped pizzas.

Timing and the obvious note: leaving Rainier ~5:30 PM puts Astoria at ~8:30. Everyone is under 21 — this is a food stop only; minors are welcome in the dining areas, nobody orders beer. Call ahead to confirm the kitchen serves that late; otherwise use the co-op below.

Day 2 · Aug 9 · late Astoria backup

Astoria Co+op deli

2350 Marine Drive · open daily until 9 PM
$7–16 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Vegetarian/vegan hot bar, salad bar, soup, or grab-and-go wrap. Wraps $9.99; burritos $10.99; hot bar $12.99/lb.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Check the day's case for chicken or fish; if none is available, use vegetarian hot-bar food. Selection rotates daily.

Why: if the Rainier exit slips past 5:30 or Fort George's kitchen is closed, this is faster and open later than many Astoria kitchens — grab it before 9 and eat en route to Seaside.

Day 3 · Aug 10 · Tillamook (REPLACES Doryland)

Tillamook Creamery food hall

4165 US-101 N, Tillamook · free factory tour · food hall closes before the shop — arrive by 1
About $12–18 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Grilled cheese, cheese pizza, or mac & cheese — everything cheese-forward is safe. Ice cream after.

🐔 Two chicken/fish diners

Check the menu for chicken strips; the burgers are BEEF — off-limits. Fallback: the same cheese dishes plus ice cream.

Why the swap: the creamery sits right on 101 (no Pacific City detour), and lunch + the free factory tour fit one 50-minute stop on the speed-run day. Group estimate: ~$50–70 for four with ice cream.

Day 6 · Aug 13 · pack in Salinas

El Charrito

122 W Market Street · opens 6 AM Mon–Sat, 7 AM Sunday
Est. $8–14 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Bean-and-cheese or chile relleno burrito. Ask whether beans and rice contain lard or chicken stock; choose whole pinto beans when needed.

🐔 Two chicken/fish diners

Pollo guisado burrito: shredded chicken, tomato, onions, and jalapeños. Avoid every beef and pork filling.

Timing: buy before leaving Salinas and picnic later. Scenic backup: Café Kevah, where vegetarian sandwiches/salads run $17.75–22 and chicken salad is $24.75.

Day 7 · Aug 14 · dinner near Griffith

HomeState (Los Feliz)

4624 Hollywood Boulevard · open into the evening; confirm closing time
$12–20 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Bean-and-cheese, egg, or veggie tacos on house-made flour tortillas, plus queso and chips. Ask whether the refried beans are cooked with lard or meat stock before ordering.

🐔 Two chicken/fish diners

Chicken tinga tacos or a chicken taco plate. Skip the brisket and any bacon breakfast tacos.

Timing: Griffith sunset is ~7:45 PM and there is no flight tonight — eat in Los Feliz after the observatory, then drive to the LAX-area motel. Backup that keeps late hours: Fred 62 diner on Vermont Avenue (veggie burgers and chicken sandwiches; confirm current hours).

Day 8 · Aug 15 · LAX quick breakfast

Grab-and-go near the airport

Hotel lobby / early-opening coffee shops near LAX · airside options after security
$6–12 each
🌱 Two vegetarians

Oatmeal, bagels with cream cheese, fruit, yogurt, or muffins — all easy finds at the motel breakfast, a nearby coffee shop, or airside cafes.

🐟 Two chicken/fish diners

Same grab-and-go picks; chicken or fish is rarely on breakfast menus. Avoid bacon and sausage breakfast sandwiches.

Timing: the car goes back ~7:30 AM and DL2568 boards for a 10:59 AM departure — do not plan a sit-down meal. Eat whatever the motel offers or buy airside; leftovers from last night's grocery stash also count as breakfast.
